01. About me

I like problems where the model is only half the work. Getting a recommender to rank well is fun; getting it to filter a million-track catalog in the time it takes to blink is the part I really enjoy.

My projects span retrieval-augmented generation, two-stage recommenders, multimodal deep learning and computer vision, and I build the systems around them too: FastAPI services, Redis caches, Docker and Kubernetes deployments, and the React front ends people actually click on.

I care about honest evaluation (leakage-free splits, realistic transaction costs, load tests with real percentiles), clean modular code, and shipping things that work end to end.
