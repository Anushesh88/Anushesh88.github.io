01. About me

I'm a final-year B.Tech (Hons.) in Instrumentation Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, based in Kharagpur, India. Instrumentation taught me to care about signals, sensors and measurement, and that's how I approach ML: get the data pipeline right, measure honestly, then ship.

I've done two internships. At Dewinter Optical I turned legacy microscopy analysis software into an AI-driven system with segmentation models and an annotation tool. At Zip Softwares I built core modules of a hospital management system, from dashboards to payments.

Outside work I build end-to-end projects in retrieval-augmented generation, recommender systems and multimodal deep learning, and I ran the finances for the National Students' Space Challenge.
